About me

  • I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), working with Prof. Zhe Wang.
  • My research interests lie in Urban Energy System Modeling and Geographic Information System. My work connects geospatial computing with urban building energy modelling, renewable energy systems, and urban energy resilience.
  • I received my Ph.D. in Civil Engineering from HKUST in 2026. During my doctoral studies, I was a visiting Ph.D. student in Urban Studies and Planning at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology. I previously received an M.Eng. from Wuhan University and a B.Sc. from Wuhan University of Technology, both in Geographic Information Science.
